Groupy

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

Groupy is a camera and group chat app made by Nalin Murrell, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Groupy collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: groupysupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), name, username and birthday. A mobile number is optional. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the account identifier and email they share with us. We also record when you accepted the Terms of Use.
  • Profile: an optional profile photo and profile background. Your name, username and profile pictures are visible to other Groupy users. Your birthday and mobile number are private to you.
  • What you send: chats, photos, videos (snaps), voice notes, reactions, replies and edits, which chats or snaps were saved, and notices such as screenshots taken in a chat. We record when a snap has been opened so the sender can see it.
  • Friends and groups: your friends and friend requests, the groups you're in, people you've blocked, private nicknames you give friends, your chat and notification settings, and reports you file.
  • Calls: voice and video calls are carried live and are not recorded. We keep a record of each call (who was in it, when, and how long).
  • Device information: a push notification token so we can notify you of new messages and calls. Snaps are downloaded to your phone ahead of time so they open instantly, and are kept in the app's cache there.
  • Stored only on your phone: accounts you've signed in with on that phone (to show the "welcome back" screen) and, if you choose "Save Login Info", a login token in the iPhone Keychain so you can log back in with one tap. These never leave the device.
  • Groupy Score: the number of photos you've sent. You, your friends and people in your group chats can see it.

Advertising

Groupy shows one sponsored row in the chat list, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, information about ads shown and tapped, approximate location derived from your IP address, and diagnostic data. Groupy does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we don't share your messages, contacts or profile with advertisers. In the EEA, UK and other places where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How we use it

  • To run Groupy: create your account, deliver what you send to the people you send it to, show who's opened it, connect calls and send notifications.
  • To keep people safe: review reports, remove content and accounts that break the Terms of Use, and enforce blocks.
  • To show ads in the sponsored row, as described above.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use your messages to target ads.

Who we share it with

Only with the people you choose to send things to, and with the services that run Groupy for us:

  • Supabase: hosting for our database, file storage, sign-in and server code.
  • Apple: push notifications and Sign in with Apple.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, AdMob ads, and email delivery for sign-up and password reset codes.
  • LiveKit: carrying voice and video calls.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, for example by reporting illegal content to the authorities.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and what you've sent for as long as your account exists, or until you or the sender deletes it. Opened snaps are hidden in the app but may stay on our servers until the chat or the sender's account is deleted. Reports are kept so we can act on them. Deleted data may remain in backups for a short time before it's overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your name, username, birthday, mobile number and email in Settings.
  • Delete your own messages and snaps from a chat at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, your messages and your files.
  • Turn off notifications per chat, or in iOS Settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to some uses. Email groupysupport@gmail.com and we'll help.

Security

Everything Groupy sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and access to chats and files is limited to their members. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so they're stored on our servers in a form we could access if we needed to, for example to review a report.

Children

Groupy is for people aged 13 and over, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
