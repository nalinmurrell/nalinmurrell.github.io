Using Groupy

Groupy lets you send photos, videos, chats and voice notes to friends and groups, and call them. By creating an account you agree to these terms. You must be at least 13 years old.

Zero tolerance for abuse

There is no place on Groupy for objectionable content or abusive people. You agree not to post or send:

  • harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation
  • nudity or sexual content, and never anything sexual involving anyone under 18
  • violence, self-harm encouragement, or glorifying harm
  • hate speech or attacks on people for who they are
  • spam, scams, or anything illegal
  • someone else's private information or images without their permission

Accounts that break these rules are removed, and illegal content is reported to the authorities.

Reporting and blocking

Long-press any message from someone else and tap Report, or report a person from your friends list or a group's members. Reports are reviewed, and we act on them, usually within 24 hours, by removing the content and the account behind it. You can block anyone: they won't be able to message you, call you or add you, and they won't be told.

Your content

You own what you send. You give Groupy permission to store it and deliver it to the people you send it to; that's all it's used for. Don't send anything you don't have the right to share. "Opened" snaps are hidden in the app but may stay on our servers until the chat or your account is deleted.

Groupy+

Groupy+ is an optional subscription that removes ads: $2.99 a month or $29.99 a year (or the equivalent in your currency). Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. It renews automatically at the same price and length unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before it. Manage or cancel it any time in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Groupy+ until the end of the period you've paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what happens on your account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your profile, your messages and your files.
