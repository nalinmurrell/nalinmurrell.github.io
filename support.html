Support

Need help, or need us to look at something? Email groupysupport@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day.

Report someone or something

In the app, long-press any message from someone else and tap Report, or report a person from your friends list or a group's members. Reports are reviewed, usually within 24 hours. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Block someone

Long-press their chat in the chat list → Manage Friendship → Block. They can't message you, call you or add you, and they aren't told. Unblock them in Settings → Blocked.

Forgot your password

On the Log In screen, tap Forgot your password?. We'll email you a code to set a new one. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

Mute a chat

Long-press the chat → Chat and Notification Settings → Notification Settings.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, your messages and your files. If you can't get into your account, email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.
